VOLC 500 — Volcanology: A Coursework MSc Equivalent

A four-term program in physical volcanology with a Hawaiʻi specialization, designed for a student holding an MSc in earth science with a deep geophysics background. Instructor of record: Claude (seminar, problem sets, data labs, oral examinations).

UWD tilt, schematic — episodes 45–50 sawtooth; right margin: the episode 51 run-up, interruptions included

Duration
600–700 h · ~2 yr part-time or 1 yr half-time
Format
Readings → problem sets → data labs on live Hawaiian data → 45-min oral exam per module
Prerequisites
MSc earth science; continuum mechanics; signal processing; Python
Spine texts
Parfitt & Wilson · Modeling Volcanic Processes · Segall · Encyclopedia of Volcanoes 2e · USGS PP 1801 (free)

Term I — Foundations · ~160 h

Move fast where the geophysics transfers; go slow where it doesn't. M2 is the gap term — budget double time.

Assessment

  • Per module: graded problem set, reviewed lab notebook, 45-min oral exam (Socratic; no multiple choice)
  • Per term: 2,500–3,500-word synthesis paper
  • Capstone: full manuscript + mock peer review

Capstone Topics (choose one)

  • Muography feasibility for Kīlauea — first for any Hawaiian volcano
  • Failure-time statistics of the episode cycles
  • Open episode + gas-piston catalog (automated)
  • Fountain-height / tilt-drop / volume scaling
  • Per-episode TROPOMI SO₂ mass balance

Optional Intensives

  • UH Hilo CSAV international volcano-monitoring course — 8 wk, open to unaffiliated applicants, apply by Dec 1
  • HVO volunteer program — ≥3-month placement, individual research projects
  • HVO Volcano Awareness Month lectures — every January
