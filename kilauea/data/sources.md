## Primary sources (most load-bearing)

- HVO daily updates: https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/kilauea/volcano-updates
- HANS notice archive for Kīlauea: https://volcanoes.usgs.gov/hans-public/volcano/hi3
  (individual notices at https://volcanoes.usgs.gov/hans-public/notice/DOI-USGS-HVO-...)
- USGS FAQ on the summit eruption:
  https://www.usgs.gov/observatories/hvo/frequently-asked-questions-about-kilaueas-summit-eruptions
- Volcano Watch (weekly HVO column), especially: "A 'window' into forecasting
  fountaining episodes", "So what on Earth... is a 'gas piston'?", "The pressure is
  on", "Inter-episode rumblings", "Twenty episodes and counting", "Hawaiʻi Five-O!
  Fifty fountaining episodes", "One year of Kīlauea's episodic summit fountaining"
- Peer-reviewed background: Chouet & Dawson 2015 JGR (gas-piston seismic source);
  Patrick et al. 2016 EPSL (gas pistoning); Colella & Dieterich 2015 (Puʻuʻōʻō
  1983–86 tilt/seismicity cycles); EGU26-9963 (2024–26 seismic/geodetic phases)
- News mirrors used where USGS pages were inaccessible: bigislandvideonews.com,
  bigislandnow.com, mauinow.com, kauainownews.com, hawaiinewsnow.com,
  staradvertiser.com, watchers.news, hawaii-guide.com
