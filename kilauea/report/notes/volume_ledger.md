## Caveats

- LERZ: 0.8 km³ is the round bulk figure chosen to match collapse; 0.9–1.4 km³ DRE is
  the better-constrained refinement.
- 2023 per-eruption volumes conflict between sources (18.6 vs 15.9 M m³ assignment);
  Jan–Mar 2023 total not found.
- "35% refilled" statements are from varying dates and crater-fill volume ≠ cumulative
  erupted volume (drainback, spatter, geometry) — do not sum naively.
- Puʻuʻōʻō 1998 cumulative point not published; 2.4 km³ is a flagged interpolation.
