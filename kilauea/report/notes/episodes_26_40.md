## Era trends (26→40)

- Pauses lengthened: 6–10 d (eps 25–29) → 16+ d common from ep 30; ~22.3 d before ep 36.
- Tilt amplitudes grew with pause length: ~11.5 µrad (pre-26) → ~22 µrad (ep 31) →
  31.6 µrad inflation pre-39 / 33.1 µrad deflation in 38.
- Tallest episodes were shortest (ep 34: 6 h @ 1,300 ft; ep 35: 7.5 h @ 1,500 ft record;
  ep 36: <5 h; ep 39: 5.9 h @ 1,400 ft) — higher effusion rate, shorter-lived.
- Precursor style shifted to counted overflow–drainback sequences: ~7 (ep 32) → 104
  (ep 33) → >120 gas-piston events (ep 34) → 179–180 (ep 36 record) → 73 (ep 37) →
  ~5 h continuous overflow (ep 38) → 4-day precursory phase (ep 40). Precursory phases
  lengthened from hours to days as pauses lengthened.
- Forecast performance: nearly every onset inside the (sometimes adjusted) window;
  clearest miss ep 29 (~1–2 d late). Mid-pause adjustments: ep 36 (slowing inflation),
  ep 37 (stall: overflows stopped + brief deflation, window shifted).
- Anomalies: ep 30 new fissure via shallow dike (precursory S-rim quakes); ep 31
  inclined fountains; ep 33 instant-max onset; ep 35 tephra on Hwy 11; ep 38 triple
  fountain / largest volume; ep 39 anniversary onset; ep 28 S-vent burial.
