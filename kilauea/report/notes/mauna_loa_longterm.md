# Agent sweep notes: Mauna Loa long-term output & the decline question

(Recorded from research-agent output, 2026-07-13. Quotes near-verbatim via search
extraction; paraphrases flagged in agent transcript. Verify against primary PDFs:
Lipman 1995 GM092p0045; Lipman & Moore 1996 JGR; Lockwood 1995 GM092p0081;
Wanless et al. 2006 G-Cubed; Pietruszka 2024 egae121.)

## 1. Lipman "declining growth" thesis

- Lipman 1995 (Mauna Loa Revealed, AGU GM 92): coastal lava-accumulation <2 mm/yr
  since 10–100 ka vs isostatic subsidence 2.4–2.6 mm/yr → net subaerial growth ≈ 0;
  "Mauna Loa is no longer growing vigorously or even maintaining its size above sea
  level" (Lipman & Moore 1996, Hilo drill core: 239 m of lava since 100–120 ka
  balancing submergence; rates 0.6–4.3 mm/yr, pulses at ~1.3 and ~10 ka).
- Drowned reef at 13.9 ka (submergence outpaced growth).
- No single "% decline" figure; framed as accumulation ≈ subsidence.
- No formal rebuttal found. Qualifiers: Wanless 2006 — 1877 submarine eruption much
  larger than thought (+10% to recent volcanism; subaerial accounting undercounts);
  modern geophysics (2002+ inflation, 2022 eruption, rapid reinflation) shows robust
  ongoing deep supply.
- DePaolo & Stolper 1996: plume-transit growth model; tholeiitic→alkalic transition
  accompanies accumulation-rate drop 9→2 m/ka (the eventual endgame signature).

## 2. Shield-stage status — confirmed NOT post-shield

- "Holocene eruptions of Mauna Loa have all produced tholeiitic lavas and eruption
  rates have remained relatively high. Thus, the volcano has probably not yet entered
  the postshield stage" (Wanless et al. 2006). The two ~15–20 ka submarine alkalic
  radial-vent lavas are evolved (~1.0 GPa fractionation) bypass magmas, not a stage
  transition. (A Wikipedia claim of post-shield transition 200–250 ka is inconsistent
  with primary literature — treat as error.)

## 3. Surface ages (Lockwood/Trusdell mapping)

- ~90% of surface <4,000 yr (Lockwood & Lipman 1987; USGS Geology & History page).
- 35–40% of the surface (1,800–2,060 km²) covered within the past 1,000–1,500 yr
  (Trusdell & Lockwood map series).
- ~500 flows mapped; 355 ¹⁴C dates → 170 dated flows — "most detailed prehistoric
  eruptive chronology of any volcano on Earth" (Lockwood 1995).

## 4. Radiocarbon eruption-rate structure

- Two identified cycles of 1,500–2,000 yr each: summit-dominant (long-lived lava
  lakes, overflows, rift compression) alternating with rift-dominant phases.
- HIATUS: ~7,000–6,000 BP Mauna Loa "largely inactive" — ~1,000-yr mid-shield quiet,
  cause unknown, unmatched at other non-post-shield Hawaiian volcanoes — followed by
  full recovery.
- Pānaewa pulse ~1.3–1.5 ka: largest flow in the Hilo area; lava-delta volume alone
  2–3 km³ — "much greater than the largest historical Mauna Loa eruptions."
- Rates: past 3,000 yr average ≈ 1 eruption/6 yr; 1843–1950 ≈ 1/3.25 yr (above
  trend); post-1950 ≈ 4 in 75 yr (far below); 1984–2022 = 38 yr, longest recorded.

## 5. Anticorrelation with Kīlauea

- Klein 1982 (JVGR): longer Mauna Loa repose statistically correlated with Kīlauea
  activity.
- PP 1806/PP 1801: ~0.11 km³/yr "interpreted as the steady supply rate from the
  mantle to both volcanoes combined throughout historical time"; Kīlauea's inferred
  supply "diminished to one-tenth of that value after 1840, in part because of
  increase in the activity of Mauna Loa beginning in 1843."
- Keanakākoʻi era (Kīlauea supply 1–2% of normal, 1500–1820): Mauna Loa flows
  interbedded with the tephra — ML active during Kīlauea's low. Keanakākoʻi magmas
  primitive (Fo89) = low-supply signature (Lynn et al. 2017 CMP).
- Explicit "Mauna Loa quiet during ʻAilāʻau era (~1000–1500)": NOT FOUND (implied by
  framework only).
- Pietruszka 2024: decadal alternation via shared source; Nb/Y minimum at Kīlauea
  ~2000–2010 coincides with Mauna Loa seismicity/inflation onset (2002).

## 6. Vital statistics

- Emergence above sea level ~300 ka; seafloor inception 0.6–1 Ma; shield built mainly
  700–100 ka; volume 65,000–80,000 km³ (Earth's largest active volcano).
- Kamaʻehuakanaloa (Lōʻihi): preshield→shield transition; emergence in ~10–100 kyr.
- No published date for Mauna Loa's post-shield transition (order tens of kyr+).
- Hawaiian volcano lifespan ~3–5 Myr; plate motion ~7–10 cm/yr.
