## Era pattern observations (1→25)

- Standard life-cycle: pause + re-inflation → precursory low-level activity (glow,
  spatter, dome fountains, small overflows, gas pistoning) minutes-to-days → abrupt
  transition to high fountaining with simultaneous tremor surge + switch to rapid
  deflation → abrupt end (often both vents within minutes) at deflation→inflation flip.
- HVO generalization: "low-level precursory activity has lasted from a few hours to a
  few days and can include spatter from the north and/or south vents, small dome
  fountains, and lava overflowing from one or both vents."
- Tilt threshold: ~6 µrad triggered eps 2–3; doubled to ~12 µrad after ep 3's large
  deflation. Episode deflations: ~6.5 µrad (10), ~10 µrad (11), 14/11 µrad (16).
- Tremor: eruptive tremor "constant, loud, unmistakable"; persistent low-level tremor
  between episodes; PULSING tremor can precede (ep 20: from ~9 h ahead, 3-min pulses
  ~8/hr); gas-piston drainbacks produce tremor spikes (eps 14, 15, 18, 24).
- Hydrogen flames observed from vents during pauses (Jan; before ep 19; imaged May 21).
- Stats through ep 20 (Volcano Watch): durations 4.5 h–8.5 d (11 of 20 under 24 h);
  repose 16 h–12 d; tallest >1,000 ft (15, 16); flows 816 acres, ~200 ft thick locally.
- Trend: heights grew (~80 m ep 1 → routine 300 m from March), durations shortened
  (multi-day → 4.5–10 h), pauses lengthened (2–6 d → 4.5–10 d). Eruption onset itself
  had only ~30 min precursory seismicity.
- Anomalies: ep 3 (8.5 d, 13M m³, threshold doubled); ep 17 (S-only, no high phase);
  ep 18 (stall + 2 false starts); ep 20 (shortest); ep 23 (dual 800–1,000 ft, 6 h);
  ep 24 (16,500-ft plume).
