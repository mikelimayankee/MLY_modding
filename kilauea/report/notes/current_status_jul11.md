# Agent sweep notes: monitoring reconstruction, July 5–11, 2026

(Pause after episode 50, awaiting episode 51. Recorded from research-agent output,
2026-07-11 ~afternoon HST. Quotes surfaced via web-search extraction — direct fetches
403'd; minor wording drift possible. UWD = Uēkahuna tiltmeter; SDH tiltmeter offline
due to storm/power outages and deep tephra blocking access SW of the caldera.)

## Context

- Episode 50 ended abruptly 5:10 p.m. HST Jun 27, 2026 after 7 h of continuous
  north-vent fountaining; summit deflation totaled 15.3 µrad on UWD.
- Jun 28 forecast: episode 51 likely Jul 6–12. Jul 1–2: deflationary interruption →
  window revised Jul 7–14; reinflation resumed Thursday evening Jul 2.
- Jul 4: recovery 7.8 µrad, "steadily climbing"; window Jul 9–14.

## Day-by-day

- **Jul 5**: recovery ~9.3 µrad, gradually climbing. North-vent glow brightened
  through the night, more consistent than prior nights; moderate–bright continuous
  south-vent glow. "Low-level seismic tremor continued during the current pause, with
  resumed, intermittent tremor bursts... likely in association with gas piston cycles
  in the eruptive vents." Caldera earthquake activity low. Window Jul 9–13, with the
  caveat that slowed inflation or deflation may delay onset.
- **Jul 6 — deflationary interruption**: "Summit reinflation proceeded steadily from
  Thursday evening, July 2 through yesterday evening, July 5, but it has stopped or is
  deflating across the summit this morning." Recovery 10.2 µrad; UWD flat since
  midnight. Tremor bursts every 10–15 min (gas pistons).
- **Jul 7**: slow deflation since Jul 6; recovery 9.5 µrad (−0.7). Switched back to
  inflation the afternoon of Jul 7. Overflight: incandescent lava surface deep within
  the SOUTH vent; no lava visible in the north vent.
- **Jul 8 — window pushed to Jul 11–15**: reinflating since Jul 7 p.m.; recovery
  10.2 µrad (+0.6). "Two brief transitions to slow deflationary tilt... pushed back
  the forecast window." South vent variable glow; north vent dark most of night, glow
  returning ~3 a.m.
- **Jul 9**: recovery 12.1 µrad (+2.0). Glow both vents. Night of Jul 8–9: south-vent
  bright-glow periods after 3 a.m. correlated with tremor spikes — "appears to mark
  the onset of gas piston activity in the south vent"; "Continuous, low-level seismic
  tremor transitioned to cyclic tremor bursts every 5–10 minutes around 3:00 a.m....
  These spikes appear to mark drainback events associated with gas piston cycles in
  the south vent." SO2 typical pause range 1,000–5,000 t/d; ERZ below detection.
  Window Jul 11–15.
- **Jul 10 — window tightened to Jul 11–13; first "rare spatter"**: recovery
  ~14.1 µrad (+2.0). "Strong glow at both the north and south eruptive vents, and rare
  spatter from the north vent... indicating the magma column is relatively high in
  both vents." Low-level tremor with occasional gas-piston bursts. "Episode 51 will
  occur sometime between July 11 and 13 if there are no additional deflationary
  events." ERZ/SWRZ seismicity and deformation low.
- **Jul 10 night → Jul 11 — precursory spatter escalates, then tilt reverses**:
  - "Intermittent spatter bursts, 5–10 minutes apart, began from the north vent just
    after 11 p.m. HST on July 10"; "spatter bursts became nearly continuous just after
    midnight on July 11." Spatter accumulation built a small cone on the north vent rim.
  - Official Jul 11 status (9:09 a.m. HST): "Kīlauea volcano is erupting small
    precursory spatter bursts." Alert ADVISORY / YELLOW.
  - Tilt: "The summit recorded inflationary tilt over the past day but flattened
    around 4:00 a.m. HST this morning and then shifted to deflation in the past few
    hours." Recovery "about 15.3 microradians on the Uēkahuna (UWD) tiltmeter, an
    increase of 1.2 microradians since yesterday morning" — i.e. recovery reached
    ~100% of episode 50's 15.3 µrad deflation exactly when it flattened.
  - "Nearly continuous spatter started at about midnight Saturday from the north
    vent, but became less vigorous after slow summit deflation began."
  - Forecast (verbatim): "Current forecast models suggest that lava fountaining
    episode 51 is likely to start between now and July 13, but most likely today or
    tomorrow. However, if the current deflation is sustained, then Monday, July 13 is
    more likely."
  - Episode 51 had NOT started as of the latest reporting found (Jul 11 morning update).
