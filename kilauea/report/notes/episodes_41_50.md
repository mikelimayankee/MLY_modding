## Era trends (41→50)

- Two-vent era ended: eps 41–43 dual fountains (1,300–1,770 ft); from ep 44 ONLY the
  north vent fountains; south vent = precursory spatter/overflows, gas jetting, flames.
  North vent initiates ~90% of precursory phases (ep 50's south-vent initiation notable).
- Heights stepped down after ep 43 record: 44–49 ~650–870 ft; ep 50's 1,030 ft tallest since.
- Durations converged to 7–9.5 h, shortening toward the end (49: 7.5 h, 50: 7.0 h),
  abrupt endings the rule.
- Deflation per episode shrank from ~31–34 µrad (41–43) to consistent ~14–17.6 µrad
  (44–50) — matching required re-inflation ~15–17 µrad between episodes (HVO's tilt
  "gas gauge"). Seismic-velocity changes added to forecasts from mid-May.
- Pauses: 22–30 d (42–44; ep 44's 30 d longest of eruption, aggravated by storm noise
  in tilt), then 9–17 d for 45–50. Post-50 pause ran long (14+ d) due to repeated
  deflationary interruptions.
- Precursor lead times varied wildly: ~1 h (43) to 6 d/~75 overflows (44); ~55 h (45),
  ~31 h (46), hours (47), ~35 h/29 overflows (48), ~5.4 h dome fountains (49), ~25 h (50).
- Forecasts: all ten inside HVO's FINAL windows, but 42, 44, 48 (and 51) pushed back
  after deflation events / flat-tilt stalls. Sharp deflations reliably delayed onsets;
  return to inflation treated as a precondition. Onset always = sharp tremor increase +
  switch to rapid deflationary tilt.

## Pause after episode 50 → July 11, 2026

- Ended 5:10 p.m. Jun 27; WATCH→ADVISORY, ORANGE→YELLOW. Choppy recovery with several
  deflationary interruptions (Jul 1–2, Jul 6–7). Jul 9: 12.1 µrad recovered (+2/day).
- Low-level tremor with gas-piston bursts throughout; glow both vents.
- Forecast evolution: Jul 6–12 → Jul 7–14 → Jul 9–14/13 → Jul 11–15 → Jul 11–13.
- Jul 11: north-vent spatter bursts from ~11 p.m. Jul 10, nearly continuous after
  midnight, building a small spatter cone; tilt flattened ~4:00 a.m., shifted to
  deflation; HVO: onset "most likely today or tomorrow," Monday Jul 13 if deflation
  sustained. Episode 51 NOT started as of the Jul 11 morning update.
